About the project

Students who work at a car rental branch pick up shifts across three overlapping daily slots. Sixt Students gives them one place to request those shifts, upload their contracts and see what they have earned. It gives the branch admin one place to staff the week, record who actually showed up, and pay everyone the right amount at the end of the month.

I built it on my own, from the database up: a React and TypeScript client, an Express API on PostgreSQL, contract files in S3, and Docker to run it all. Shifts are generated every night by a scheduled job, and each student’s hours and pay are calculated from the shifts they actually worked.

Why I built it

I used to work as a student at the Sixt branch at Zagreb airport, and the way we handled shifts always felt more complicated than it needed to be. Shift requests went through Google Forms and WhatsApp. Contracts had to be handed in person at the boss’s office. Every student kept their own count of the hours they worked during the month and hoped it matched the manager’s.

The branch manager had it worse. He built each week’s schedule by hand and added up everyone’s hours himself at the end of the month. That’s slow, repetitive work, and he had better things to spend his time on.

So I built Sixt Students. Students get one place to request shifts, upload contracts and see what they’ve earned. The manager gets one place to staff the week, record attendance and run payroll, with the shifts created automatically. I wanted working at Sixt to feel more professional for students, and I wanted the manager to get his time back for the work that actually needs him.

What each side of the app does

Students 5 screens

  • Request shiftsPick open morning, middle or afternoon slots on the weekly calendar.
  • Upload contractsSend the monthly contract up to three months ahead.
  • Track earningsHours and pay by month, shift type and weekday.
  • Race to a goalSet a monthly goal and see how you rank against teammates.
  • See your scheduleCheck which shifts you’ve been assigned this week and which requests are still pending.

Admin 6 screens

  • Staff the weekAssign students from the requests on each shift.
  • Automatic shift staffingStaff a shift with just one click
  • Record attendanceMark who showed up and correct start and end times.
  • Chase contractsSee whose contract is missing and send a reminder.
  • Manage student accountsCreate new students and manage their accounts
  • Close payrollCheck the month against the budget and export it to CSV.
