About the project

Students who work at a car rental branch pick up shifts across three overlapping daily slots. Sixt Students gives them one place to request those shifts, upload their contracts and see what they have earned. It gives the branch admin one place to staff the week, record who actually showed up, and pay everyone the right amount at the end of the month.

I built it on my own, from the database up: a React and TypeScript client, an Express API on PostgreSQL, contract files in S3, and Docker to run it all. Shifts are generated every night by a scheduled job, and each student’s hours and pay are calculated from the shifts they actually worked.

What each side of the app does

Students 5 screens

  • Request shiftsPick open morning, middle or afternoon slots on the weekly calendar.
  • Upload contractsSend the monthly contract up to three months ahead.
  • Track earningsHours and pay by month, shift type and weekday.
  • Race to a goalSet a monthly goal and see how you rank against teammates.

Admin 6 screens

  • Staff the weekAssign students from the requests on each shift.
  • Record attendanceMark who showed up and correct start and end times.
  • Chase contractsSee whose contract is missing and send a reminder.
  • Close payrollCheck the month against the budget and export it to CSV.
